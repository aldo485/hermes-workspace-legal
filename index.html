Hermes Workspace

Private Google Workspace assistance for the owner (GitHub: aldo485).

What this app does

Hermes Workspace connects a locally operated Hermes assistant to the owner's Google account. It supports finding and summarizing information, drafting and sending email, organizing files, working with documents and spreadsheets, and managing calendars when the owner requests those tasks.

This is a personal-use desktop integration. It is not a public sign-up service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Access requested

  • Gmail: read messages, send messages, and modify mailbox data.
  • Drive: access and manage files permitted by the owner's Google authorization.
  • Calendar: access and manage calendars and events.
  • Docs and Sheets: access and edit documents and spreadsheets.
  • Contacts: read-only access.

These describe granted capabilities. Individual consequential actions remain subject to the owner's operating instructions and approvals.

Where processing happens

Hermes runs on the owner's computer. Content used in a task may also be sent to the AI providers and tools configured by the owner. See the privacy notice for storage, processing, and revocation details.
