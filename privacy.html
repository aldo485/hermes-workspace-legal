Privacy notice

Operator: the owner (GitHub: aldo485). Application: Hermes Workspace. Intended audience: the operator's personal use.

Data access and purpose

The app accesses Google data needed for the owner's requested assistance: email and mailbox metadata, Drive files and metadata, calendars and events, documents, spreadsheets, and contact information. It uses that data to retrieve, summarize, draft, organize, or perform owner-authorized actions.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's local Hermes profile. Task content and results may persist in Hermes conversation history, logs, memory, local files, backups, and destinations selected by the owner. This deployment does not promise automatic deletion after a fixed period. Revoking access prevents future authorized API use but does not erase previously stored material.

AI providers and other recipients

Relevant task content may be transmitted to the configured AI inference provider to produce a response. Content may also be sent to a tool, service, or recipient when required by an owner-authorized task, such as sending an email. Local execution does not mean all processing remains on the computer.

Data-use commitments

Google user data will be used only for the disclosed personal-assistance features. It will not be sold, used for advertising, or used to train a general-purpose AI model. Transfers will be limited to providing the owner-authorized features or other uses allowed by Google's applicable policies.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and removal

The owner can review or revoke the connection in Google Account connections. To remove retained copies, the owner must separately remove the relevant Hermes history, memory, files, backups, and any copies retained by configured services under their controls.
